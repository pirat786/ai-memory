Privacy Policy for AI Memory

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. Local-First Data Storage

AI Memory stores your project notes, preferences, and memory entries locally on your computer in an SQLite database. We do not operate any central servers and do not collect, sell, or share your data.

2. Google Drive API Usage

If you choose to connect your Google account, AI Memory requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope. This grants access exclusively to a hidden application-specific folder in your personal Google Drive to store and synchronize your encrypted/compressed memory snapshot (ai-memory-sync.json.gz) across your own devices.

AI Memory cannot read, modify, or delete any of your personal files or folders on Google Drive.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete any memory entry inside the application or disconnect Google Drive sync at any time by removing your local configuration or revoking access in your Google Account permissions.
